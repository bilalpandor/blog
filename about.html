About Me

I'm a polyglot software engineer with over 5 years of experience contributing to diverse projects spanning multiple languages, technology stacks, and development domains. I'm passionate about software craftsmanship, fostering positive teamwork, and actively contributing to shaping the future of technology.

My experience spans across the full technology stack, with substantial expertise in backend development using functional Java (Java 17, Vert.x, RxJava) and C# (.NET), building RESTful microservices hosted on AWS and Azure. I also have frontend experience with React, Angular, and modern JavaScript/TypeScript in Node.js environments.

I have hands-on knowledge of CI/CD, containerization, and orchestration tools (Jenkins, GitLab, Docker, Kubernetes, ECS), and I focus heavily on software quality through Test-Driven and Behaviour-Driven development practices.

Beyond my professional work, I'm someone who is always eager to learn and excited at the potential for growth. I enjoy travelling to explore new cultures and reading history and philosophy. My passion for tech dates back to my childhood, when my father brought home our first computer. Since then, my interest has grown, and I thoroughly enjoy writing and working with software and hardware on multiple personal projects using my custom-built gaming PC.
